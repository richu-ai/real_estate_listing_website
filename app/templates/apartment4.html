Property Description

This premium apartment in Hyderabad's IT hub offers 1,800 sqft of luxurious living space with smart home features. The property features two spacious bedrooms with walk-in closets, a modern modular kitchen with breakfast counter, and a living area that opens to a balcony with city views.

The interiors showcase Italian marble flooring, premium wooden finishes, and designer lighting. The building offers amenities including a temperature-controlled swimming pool, fully-equipped gym, clubhouse, and 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance.

Strategically located in Jubilee Hills, this apartment provides easy access to major IT parks like Google, Microsoft, and Amazon campuses, as well as shopping destinations like Inorbit Mall and GVK One. Perfect for professionals seeking premium urban living in Hyderabad's most dynamic neighborhood.
