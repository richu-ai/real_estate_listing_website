Lakeside Building Lot

Lonavala, Maharashtra 1 Acre 200 ft Lake Frontage
₹75 Lakh Waterfront

Property Description

This exceptional 1-acre waterfront lot offers 200 feet of pristine lake frontage on beautiful Pawna Lake in Lonavala, Maharashtra. The property features a gently sloping terrain with a cleared building site that has been professionally graded and prepared for construction, while maintaining a natural buffer of mature hardwoods along the shoreline.

The lot has been fully approved for residential construction with all necessary permits in place, including septic system approval and dock permit. The building site offers panoramic lake views and is positioned to maximize both privacy and water access.

Pawna Lake is known for its excellent water quality, fishing, and recreational opportunities. The property includes deeded access to a shared boat launch just 0.3 km away, making it ideal for water enthusiasts. Year-round access is maintained via a private, well-maintained road.
