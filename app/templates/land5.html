Farmland Opportunity

Nashik, Maharashtra 10 Acres Productive Soil
₹1.8 Crore Agricultural

Property Description

This 10-acre agricultural parcel in Nashik's fertile grape-growing region offers excellent potential for vineyard development or continued row crop production. The land features rich, well-drained soil with a proven history of high yields, currently planted in a rotation of grapes and seasonal vegetables.

The property includes a 1,200 sq ft equipment barn with concrete floor and power, a 500 sq ft storage shed, and an operational irrigation system fed by a permitted well with excellent water yield. The land is level to gently rolling, making it ideal for mechanized farming operations.

Located just 8 km from Nashik's agricultural markets and processing facilities, this property offers convenient access to both local and national distribution networks. The area is known for its ideal grape-growing climate and established agricultural infrastructure.
