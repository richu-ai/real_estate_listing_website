Property Description

This prime 2-acre commercial development parcel is strategically located in Whitefield, Bengaluru's fastest-growing commercial corridor. The property boasts 150 feet of frontage on the Whitefield Main Road, providing excellent visibility and accessibility in this high-traffic area.

The land is zoned for mixed-use development and has received preliminary approval for a commercial complex. All utilities are available at the site boundary, including three-phase electrical service, municipal water, and high-speed internet infrastructure.

Whitefield has emerged as Bengaluru's premier commercial and IT hub, with proximity to major tech parks, shopping malls, and residential communities. This parcel represents an exceptional opportunity for developers looking to capitalize on the area's rapid growth and high demand for commercial space.
