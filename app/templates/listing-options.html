Listing Option FAQ

What's the difference between flat fee and commission?

The flat fee option requires upfront payment but no commission when sold, ideal for sellers confident in their property's value. The commission option requires no upfront cost but a percentage of the sale price, which aligns our incentives with getting you the best price.

Can I upgrade my listing package later?

Yes, you can upgrade at any time. The upgrade cost will be the difference between your current package and the new one. Many sellers start with Basic and upgrade if they don't get desired traction.

How long do listings typically remain active?

Basic and Premium listings are active for 6 months, while Elite listings remain for 12 months. All can be renewed at discounted rates if needed. Most properties sell within the initial period.

Do you offer discounts for multiple parcels?

Yes, we offer package discounts when listing 3+ parcels simultaneously. Discounts range from 10-25% depending on the number of properties and selected package.

What happens if my land doesn't sell?

We offer a free consultation to analyze market response and recommend adjustments. Options include price revision, package upgrade, or switching to our Quick Sale Program. Our success rate is 92% within the initial listing period.
