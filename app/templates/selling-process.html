Selling Your Land with Confidence

Our streamlined selling process maximizes your property's value while minimizing stress and uncertainty. Here's how we make selling your land simple and profitable.

Our Step-by-Step Selling Process

1

Initial Consultation & Valuation

We begin with a comprehensive evaluation of your property, considering location, zoning, market trends, and development potential to determine its optimal market value.

Pro Tip:
  • Gather any existing surveys, deeds, or zoning documents
  • Note any unique features or recent improvements
  • Consider your ideal timeline for selling
2

Marketing Strategy Development

We create a customized marketing plan that may include professional photography, drone footage, targeted digital advertising, and exposure to our network of developers and investors.

Pro Tip:
  • Clear any debris or obstructions for better photos
  • Highlight access points and road frontage
  • Consider seasonal factors that may affect appeal
3

Property Listing & Promotion

Your property is showcased across multiple platforms including MLS, land-specific marketplaces, and our exclusive investor network with detailed information and compelling visuals.

Pro Tip:
  • Be available for potential buyer questions
  • Consider creating a simple informational flyer
  • Highlight any development approvals or permits
4

Offer Review & Negotiation

We carefully evaluate all offers, provide detailed analysis of terms and conditions, and negotiate on your behalf to secure the best possible deal structure.

Pro Tip:
  • Consider financing options you're willing to accept
  • Understand the tax implications of your sale
  • Review comparable recent sales in your area
5

Due Diligence & Closing

We guide you through the closing process, coordinating with title companies, surveyors, and attorneys to ensure a smooth transaction that meets all legal requirements.

Pro Tip:
  • Have your identification documents ready
  • Understand your closing cost responsibilities
  • Consider 1031 exchange options if reinvesting

Seller FAQ

How much does it cost to sell my land?

Our standard commission is X% of the sale price. For our Quick Sale Program, there are no commissions - we make you a direct cash offer. Closing costs typically range from 1-3% of the sale price and may include title insurance, escrow fees, and recording fees.

How is my land's value determined?

We evaluate multiple factors including location, zoning, topography, access, utilities availability, recent comparable sales, and current market demand. For development parcels, we also consider density potential and infrastructure requirements.

What documents do I need to sell my land?

You'll typically need the deed, any existing surveys, property tax information, and documentation of any easements or restrictions. If the property has been recently inherited or is part of an estate, additional documentation may be required.

Can I sell land that's part of an inheritance?

Yes, but all heirs must agree to the sale. The process may require additional legal steps depending on whether probate has been completed. We can connect you with probate real estate specialists if needed.

How long does it take to sell land?

The average time to sell vacant land is 3-6 months, though desirable properties in hot markets may sell faster. Our Quick Sale Program can complete transactions in as little as 10 days if needed.
