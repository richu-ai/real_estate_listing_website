Urban Loft Penthouse Penthouse

Gurugram, Haryana
₹22,50,00,000

Gurugram Skyline Sanctuary

Perched on the 42nd floor of Gurugram's prestigious Horizon Tower, this 2,800 square foot penthouse redefines urban luxury in India. Designed by award-winning architect Priya Sharma, the space features floor-to-ceiling windows that frame breathtaking 270-degree views of the Delhi NCR skyline, from the Qutub Minar to the Aravalli hills.

The open-concept living area showcases exposed steel beams and polished concrete floors, softened by warm teak millwork and designer lighting. A state-of-the-art smart home system controls lighting, climate, motorized shades, and the integrated sound system throughout the residence.

The private rooftop terrace features a glass-enclosed lounge area with radiant floor heating, an outdoor kitchen with teak cabinetry, and an infinity-edge spa overlooking the city. The penthouse includes a dedicated elevator vestibule, 24/7 concierge service, and access to the building's wellness center with a 75-foot lap pool.
