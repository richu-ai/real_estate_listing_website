Concrete & Steel Residence New Construction

Austin, TX
$1,950,000

Architectural Statement

This striking residence represents the perfect marriage of brutalist concrete and warm modernism, designed by architect Carlos Mendez in 2023. The 3,500 square foot home features exposed concrete walls, steel beams, and expansive glass that create a dramatic interplay of light and shadow throughout the day.

The open floor plan centers around a double-height living space with a floating steel staircase and a 20-foot glass wall that opens completely to the outdoor living area. The home features radiant concrete floors, custom steel fixtures, and integrated smart home technology that controls lighting, security, and the home's geothermal climate system.

Situated on a 1.2 acre lot with native landscaping, the property includes a 50-foot lap pool with a cantilevered concrete deck, an outdoor kitchen with a wood-fired pizza oven, and a detached studio/office with matching architectural style. The home is LEED Platinum certified, featuring solar panels, rainwater harvesting, and native drought-resistant landscaping.
