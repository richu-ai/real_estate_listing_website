Valuation FAQs

How accurate is your valuation?

Our valuations are typically within 2-5% of the final sale price when all factors are properly considered. We use multiple data points and local expertise to ensure accuracy.

How long does a valuation take?

Our initial online valuation estimate is instant. For a more detailed valuation with a physical inspection, we typically need 24-48 hours to prepare a comprehensive report.

Is there any cost for a valuation?

No, our valuation service is completely free with no obligation. We're happy to provide this service to help you make informed decisions about your property.

What's the difference between an online valuation and in-person appraisal?

Our online valuation uses available data and algorithms for a quick estimate. An in-person appraisal involves a physical inspection of your property's condition, upgrades, and unique features for greater accuracy.

How often should I get my property valued?

If you're considering selling, we recommend a valuation every 3-6 months as market conditions can change rapidly. For general knowledge, an annual valuation is sufficient.
