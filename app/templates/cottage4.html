About This Retreat

This serene eco-friendly retreat sits directly on the banks of the sacred Ganges River in Rishikesh, offering a perfect blend of spiritual ambiance and modern comfort. Built with sustainable materials and traditional architectural techniques, the property provides a peaceful sanctuary for yoga and meditation.

The retreat features a private ghat for morning rituals, a solar-powered yoga shala with river views, and meditation decks overlooking the water. The design incorporates natural stone, reclaimed wood, and energy-efficient systems throughout.

Located in the quiet Bharat Mandir area, the retreat is just upstream from the main tourist areas, offering tranquility while being walking distance to Laxman Jhula and local cafes. The property includes a small organic garden and fruit trees.

Neighborhood

The retreat is located in one of Rishikesh's most peaceful areas, surrounded by ashrams and yoga schools. The neighborhood maintains a spiritual atmosphere while offering convenient access to cafes, markets, and the famous Laxman Jhula bridge. The area is known for its daily Ganga Aarti ceremonies and abundant wildlife including langurs and river birds.
