Minimalist Glass House Architectural

Mumbai, Maharashtra
₹18,50,00,000

Oceanfront Architectural Marvel

This stunning glass-walled residence in Mumbai's prestigious Juhu neighborhood offers panoramic views of the Arabian Sea. Designed by renowned architect Vikram Mehta, the home features floor-to-ceiling glass walls that create a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor spaces while maintaining complete privacy through smart glass technology.

The 3,200 square foot residence features four bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, each with ocean views. The open-plan living area includes a state-of-the-art kitchen with Italian marble countertops, a dining area that seats twelve, and a lounge area that opens directly to the infinity pool deck.

Smart home technology controls lighting, climate, security, and entertainment systems throughout the property. The landscaped grounds include a private beach access, meditation garden, and outdoor shower. The property is secured with 24/7 security and features a backup power system.
