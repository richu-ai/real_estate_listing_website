Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and the real estate process

General Questions

Browse our most frequently asked questions below. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

How do I get started with buying a property?

Getting started is easy! First, we recommend getting pre-approved for a mortgage so you know your budget. Then, contact one of our buyer specialists who will schedule a consultation to understand your needs, preferences, and timeline. We'll then curate a selection of properties that match your criteria and guide you through every step of the buying process.

What's your process for selling a home?

Our selling process begins with a comprehensive market analysis to determine your home's optimal listing price. We then create a customized marketing plan that includes professional photography, virtual tours, targeted advertising, and open houses. Our agents handle all showings and negotiations, keeping you informed at every stage until closing.

What are the requirements to rent a property?

Requirements vary by property, but generally include: proof of income (typically 3x the monthly rent), good credit history, rental references, and a security deposit (usually 1-2 months' rent). Some landlords may have additional requirements. Our rental specialists can help you understand the specific requirements for properties you're interested in.

What makes Premium Real Estate different?

We differentiate ourselves through our exclusive focus on premium properties, personalized service, and deep market expertise. Our agents undergo rigorous training and have access to advanced tools and analytics. We also offer innovative marketing strategies for sellers and a curated selection of properties for buyers that match their lifestyle aspirations.

How long does the home buying process typically take?

The timeline varies based on market conditions and your specific needs, but on average, the process takes 30-45 days from accepted offer to closing once you've found the right property. The search phase can take anywhere from a few weeks to several months depending on inventory and how selective you are.

What's the best time of year to sell my home?

While spring is traditionally the busiest home-selling season, the best time depends on your local market and property type. In many areas, late spring and early fall offer ideal conditions. However, with our targeted marketing strategies, we can successfully sell properties year-round. We'll analyze your specific situation to recommend the optimal listing timeline.

Do you charge fees for rental assistance?

Many of our rental listings are no-fee, with the landlord paying our commission. For exclusive listings or special circumstances, there may be a broker fee equivalent to one month's rent. We're always transparent about any fees before you commit to a property, and we'll clearly explain all costs upfront.

How do you determine property values?

We use a comprehensive comparative market analysis (CMA) that evaluates recent sales of similar properties, current market conditions, and your property's unique features. Our agents also consider factors like location desirability, property condition, and future development plans. For the most accurate valuation, we recommend scheduling a professional appraisal.

Should I buy first or sell my current home first?

This depends on your financial situation, local market conditions, and personal preferences. We offer several strategies: bridge loans, rent-back agreements, or contingent offers. During our consultation, we'll analyze your specific circumstances and market dynamics to recommend the best approach for your needs.

What improvements should I make before selling?

Focus on cost-effective improvements with high ROI: fresh paint, decluttering, professional cleaning, and minor repairs. We generally advise against major renovations as you may not recoup the investment. After evaluating your property, we'll provide specific recommendations tailored to your home and target buyers.

Can I rent with pets?

Many of our properties are pet-friendly! We can filter listings based on your pet needs. Some landlords may require additional pet deposits or monthly fees. Restrictions on breed, size, or number of pets vary by property. Be sure to discuss your pets with your rental specialist early in the process.

What areas do you serve?

We specialize in premium properties throughout the greater Miami area, including Miami Beach, Brickell, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, and surrounding neighborhoods. Our agents have hyper-local expertise in these markets. If you're looking outside our primary service area, we can refer you to trusted partners in those locations.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to provide personalized answers and guidance for your specific situation.
