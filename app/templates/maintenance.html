Cottage Maintenance Guide

Keep your cottage in perfect condition year-round with our comprehensive maintenance checklist and expert tips for seasonal care.

Seasonal Maintenance Checklist

Winter Preparation

  • Drain and winterize plumbing systems
  • Clean gutters and downspouts
  • Inspect and clean chimney
  • Seal gaps around windows and doors
  • Store outdoor furniture properly
  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Set up rodent prevention measures

Spring Refresh

  • Inspect roof for winter damage
  • Check foundation for cracks or settling
  • Clean and repair decks/porches
  • Service HVAC system
  • Test sump pump if applicable
  • Prune trees and shrubs near structure
  • Inspect and clean exterior siding

Summer Upkeep

  • Deep clean interior spaces
  • Check for pest infestations
  • Inspect and repair screens
  • Clean and treat outdoor wood surfaces
  • Test emergency generator if available
  • Clean and inspect outdoor grill area
  • Check weather stripping on doors

Fall Preparation

  • Clean and store outdoor equipment
  • Drain irrigation systems
  • Inspect and clean fireplace
  • Check attic insulation
  • Test winter equipment (snow blowers, etc.)
  • Trim branches near power lines
  • Stock up on winter supplies

Essential Maintenance Tips

Foundation Care

Regularly inspect your cottage's foundation for cracks or signs of settling. Ensure proper drainage around the perimeter to prevent water damage.

Frequency: Twice yearly (spring and fall)

Roof Maintenance

Check for missing or damaged shingles, especially after severe weather. Clean moss and debris to prevent water pooling and rot.

Signs of trouble: Water stains on ceilings, visible daylight in attic

Plumbing Checks

Inspect for leaks under sinks and around toilets. Know how to shut off main water supply in case of emergencies. Winterize pipes before freezing temperatures.

Winter tip: Let faucets drip during extreme cold

Fireplace Safety

Have chimney professionally cleaned annually. Install spark arrestor on chimney top. Keep flammable materials at least 3 feet from fireplace.

Important: Never leave fires unattended

Ventilation

Ensure proper airflow in crawl spaces and attic to prevent moisture buildup. Check and clean vents regularly to maintain air quality.

Warning sign: Musty odors or visible mold

Pest Prevention

Seal entry points, store food properly, and keep wood piles away from structure. Schedule annual pest inspections to catch issues early.

Common pests: Rodents, termites, carpenter ants
